---
title: Prerequisites
description: What you need before using Pedro Pathing with ElectroDromos
---

ElectroDromos runs on top of Pedro Pathing, so you need everything Pedro needs, plus a recent enough Pedro.

### Holonomic Drive

You have some kind of holonomic drivetrain. Pedro Pathing supports **Mecanum** and **Swerve** out of the box, but any holonomic drivetrain could be used. ElectroDromos works with any Pedro `Drivetrain`.

### Localization

The robot needs a way to know where it is on the field. Pedro Pathing supports **Pinpoint**, **OctoQuad**, **OTOS**, **Two Wheel**, **Three Wheel**, and **Three Wheel IMU**. ElectroDromos works with any Pedro `Localizer`.

### Pedro Pathing 3.0.1 or newer

ElectroDromos needs **Pedro Pathing 3.0.1** or newer and Pedro's tuning module **1.0.1**. Pedro 2 and early Pedro 3 alphas are not supported. See [Upgrading to Pedro 3](/docs/pathing/pedro3).

### REV hubs

[Voltage compensation](/docs/pathing/reference/voltage-compensation) reads the battery through the hub's voltage sensor.

### FTC SDK 11.2 or newer

ElectroDromos is built against FTC SDK 11.2 and tested with 12.0.

### Programming Platform

You **must** use **Android Studio**. Pedro Pathing and ElectroDromos do **not** work with OnBot Java or Blocks.

## Next Steps

Once you have all of these, [proceed to installation](/docs/pathing/installation).
