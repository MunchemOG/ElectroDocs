## The mark

The **D** is a racetrack seen from above: a cyan straight on the left and a red turn on the right, split by a thin gap like a battery's two leads. *Dromos* is Greek for a racecourse, which is where the name comes from. The D slants with the italic lettering.

- Keep the D's colors: cyan straight on the left, red turn on the right. Don't recolor or flip it.
- Leave clear space around the mark at least as wide as the D's straight.
- On light backgrounds, use the light wordmark. Its "DROMOS" is a darker cyan so it stays readable.

## Colors

These are ElectroLights 30686's team colors. Each one has a job:

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| <span className="whitespace-nowrap"><span className="inline-block size-4 rounded-sm border border-fd-border align-middle" style={{background:'#08090B'}} /> Black</span> | `#08090B` | The main surface: pages, panels, the mark's tile |
| <span className="whitespace-nowrap"><span className="inline-block size-4 rounded-sm border border-fd-border align-middle" style={{background:'#F4F6F7'}} /> White</span> | `#F4F6F7` | Text and "ELECTRO" in the wordmark |
| <span className="whitespace-nowrap"><span className="inline-block size-4 rounded-sm border border-fd-border align-middle" style={{background:'#19E3E8'}} /> Cyan</span> | `#19E3E8` | The one primary accent: links, focus, primary buttons, "DROMOS", ElectroDromos's compensated speed |
| <span className="whitespace-nowrap"><span className="inline-block size-4 rounded-sm border border-fd-border align-middle" style={{background:'#FF4B3A'}} /> Red</span> | `#FF4B3A` | The second channel: the battery, warnings, the red turn of the D. Never a replacement for cyan. |

In the light theme, cyan becomes `#007A80` and red `#C42E1F` so text keeps enough contrast on white.

The site signs every page with a thin **brand thread** along the top: a long cyan lead and a short red one.

## Type

- **[Chakra Petch](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chakra+Petch)** (Bold Italic in the wordmark, Semibold and Bold for headings).
- **[Barlow](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow)** for reading text.

Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.
