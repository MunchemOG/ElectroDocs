Pedro Pathing utilizes AutoTune, a robot-hosted webpage that allows for easy tuning of your robot's drivetrain, localizer, and the Foresight algorithm.

Please ensure you properly [installed](/docs/pathing/installation) Pedro Pathing and ElectroDromos before continuing.

Tuning is Pedro's. ElectroDromos never runs inside Pedro's tuners, so tune exactly as Pedro describes, then add one ElectroDromos step at the end.

<Callout title="Tip" type="info">
    Tune with a charged battery, and don't swap batteries partway through Foresight tuning.
</Callout>

The following steps will guide you through tuning your robot:

<Steps>
    <Step>
        <h3><Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/drivetrain/mecanum">Drivetrain</Link></h3>

        Setting constants for your drivetrain such as your motor names and directions.
    </Step>
    <Step>
        <h3>
            <Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/localization">Localization</Link>
        </h3>

        Tuning your localizer, which allows for your robot to know its location on the field.
    </Step>
    <Step>
        <h3><Link href="/docs/pathing/tuning/foresight">Foresight</Link></h3>

        Tuning Foresight, which is the built-in algorithm that allows your robot to follow paths accurately.
    </Step>
    <Step>
        <h3><Link href="/docs/pathing/reference/voltage-tuner">Voltage Compensation (ElectroDromos)</Link></h3>

        Finding `nominalVoltage`, the battery voltage your Pedro tune was made at, so paths drive the same on every battery.
    </Step>
</Steps>
